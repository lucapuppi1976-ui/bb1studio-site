What is Chargeback Defender?

+

A focused product offer for Shopify dispute operations. It helps structure evidence, deadlines, and review steps before submission.

Is the app already live?

+

Not yet. This package is designed so BB1 can publish the product page now and connect the live runtime later.

Does it replace legal advice?

+

No. It is an operational workflow layer, not legal advice or case-specific legal review.

Does it decide everything automatically?

+

No. Human review remains essential. The goal is cleaner operations, not blind automation.

Can the public page stay on bb1studio.com?

+

Yes. The BB1 site can act as the container while the live runtime opens later from its own app route or domain.

Does this package include a demo?

+

Yes. The demo page and sample files are already included in the HTML package.
